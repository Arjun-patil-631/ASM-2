📖 About

CodeBridge is a comprehensive placement support platform built to bridge the gap between college education and industry expectations.

Instead of switching between multiple websites for coding practice, aptitude preparation, interview resources, hackathons, and company-specific roadmaps, CareerSprint brings everything together into one intuitive application.

Whether you're preparing for your first internship or aiming for your dream company, CareerSprint provides structured guidance, daily practice, and personalized progress tracking.

🎯 Vision

To empower every student with the right resources, structured preparation, and consistent motivation to achieve their career goals.
